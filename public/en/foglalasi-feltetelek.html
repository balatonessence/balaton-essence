Booking Terms

This translation is provided for convenience. In case of any discrepancy, the Hungarian version shall prevail.
For apartment bookings, an advance payment of 50% must be paid online to finalise the booking. The remaining 50% is payable upon arrival in cash or by bank card.

1. Booking process

  • The Guest selects the preferred apartment and booking period.
  • The Guest provides the information required for the booking.
  • The booking becomes final upon online payment of the advance payment and confirmation by the Service Provider.

2. Advance payment and payment terms

For apartment bookings, an advance payment equal to 50% of the total accommodation fee must be paid online. The remaining 50% is payable upon arrival in cash or by bank card.

The advance payment applies only to apartment bookings. For other services, the payment terms displayed for the relevant service shall apply.

3. Cancellation

If the booking is cancelled at least 14 days before arrival, the full amount of the advance payment will be refunded.

If the booking is cancelled within 14 days before arrival, the advance payment is non-refundable.

4. Changes to bookings

Changes to bookings are possible only subject to availability and prior agreement. Requests for changes may be sent to info@balatonessence.com.

5. Arrival and departure

Check-in: between 3:00 PM and 5:00 PM.
Check-out: by 10:00 AM.

Different arrival times are possible only by prior arrangement.

6. No-show

If the Guest does not arrive on the booked day and does not notify the Service Provider in advance of a change or cancellation, the booking may be considered cancelled and the advance payment is non-refundable.
